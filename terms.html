Terms for TCM One accounts, course enrolment, fees, digital delivery, refunds and learning services.

1. Business and scope

The Code Munk operates the TCM One learning platform at thecodemunk.in. We provide technology courses, live workshops, project learning and the support described for each programme. These terms apply when you create an account, enrol or purchase a service. Read them together with our Privacy Policy, Refund & Cancellation Policy, Internship & Placement Policy and Certificate Policy.

2. Eligibility and accounts

Provide accurate learner and contact information and keep your login private. Purchases must be made by an adult with legal capacity to contract. Learners under 18 must have a parent or guardian contact us and authorise enrolment and the processing of their information before registration or payment. Report unauthorised account access to support.

3. Course details and fees

Check the selected course or workshop page for its syllabus, duration, delivery mode, fees, prerequisites and inclusions. Fees are billed in Indian rupees (INR); the total payable and any applicable taxes or agreed instalments must be confirmed before payment. An enquiry or demo request does not itself create a paid enrolment. Instalments or financing are available only when explicitly offered in writing; no automatic EMI or loan approval is promised.

4. Payment and digital delivery

Use only payment instructions provided through official TCM channels. Available payment methods are those actually offered when you enrol. Where a payment gateway is offered, it processes the payment under its own terms. Never send card credentials, PINs or OTPs to our team.

For payments requiring manual verification, submit the payment reference in your student dashboard. We aim to verify payment and activate the purchased access within 24 hours of receiving complete payment details. Contact support if access has not arrived within that time. A screenshot alone is not confirmation of cleared funds. Live sessions follow the batch schedule shared before payment; recordings and access duration are included only as specified for your programme. Services are delivered digitally; physical shipping is not applicable. See Course Fees, Duration & Delivery.

5. Refunds, cancellation and rescheduling

Our Refund & Cancellation Policy sets out eligibility, exceptions, how to request a refund and processing timelines. For live cohorts, the standard request window is seven calendar days from the cohort start, while less than 20% of scheduled sessions have elapsed and less than 20% of learning content has been accessed. Eligible cancellations before service delivery receive a full refund. TCM cancellation, duplicate payments and non-delivery are addressed separately in that policy. Statutory consumer remedies remain available.

6. Learning materials and conduct

Course content is provided for your personal learning. Ownership remains with TCM or the relevant authors; third-party and open-source materials retain their own licences. Do not resell restricted materials, share accounts, harass others or submit copied work as your own. We may restrict access for misuse after reviewing the issue and, where practical, giving notice and an opportunity to respond. Refund eligibility and legal rights are assessed separately.

7. Certificates and career assistance

Certificates are issued for eligible programmes according to their completion requirements and our Certificate Policy. A TCM completion certificate is not a university degree, government qualification or third-party accreditation unless that specific credential is expressly documented for the programme. Career assistance, internships and referrals depend on the published scope and available opportunities. Payment does not guarantee an internship, job, salary or interview selection.

8. Service issues and changes

Report access failures or material differences from the advertised service promptly so we can investigate and offer a remedy. Batch changes will be communicated through your registered contact details; material changes and cancellations are handled under the refund policy. Updated terms apply prospectively and do not retrospectively remove an agreed refund entitlement. Nothing here excludes rights or liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded under Indian law.

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Contact us first for enrolment disputes, payment issues or grievances. This does not limit your right to approach a competent consumer forum or authority.
