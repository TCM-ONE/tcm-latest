Admissions & Enrollment FAQ

How soon will I receive a reply after submitting?

Our admissions team monitors inquiries Monday through Saturday from 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM IST. You will typically receive an email or WhatsApp response during support hours, usually within one business day.

Can I speak directly with a mentor before enrolling?

Yes! If you are deciding between different technical tracks, message our WhatsApp line (+91 9238695500) and request a 15-minute 1-on-1 counseling call with an engineering mentor to assess your current skill level and goals.

What payment options do you support?

Payment methods are those offered through official TCM payment instructions at enrolment. The current student flow accepts a payment reference or receipt for manual verification before access is activated. Gateway payment methods and any instalment arrangement are available only when expressly offered; please confirm with admissions before paying.
