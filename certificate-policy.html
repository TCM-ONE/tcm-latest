Certificate Issuance & Verification Policy

TCM One completion certificate eligibility, issuance, corrections and verification through official support.

Effective: 15 October 2026The Code Munk · TCM One
Terms · Privacy · Refunds & cancellation · Course delivery · Internships & placements · Certificates

1. Issuer and credential

Eligible completion certificates are issued by The Code Munk (TCM One). A certificate records completion of the specified TCM learning programme. It is not a university degree, professional licence, government qualification, employment offer or assurance of placement. A partner-issued credential is included only if expressly documented for that programme.

2. Eligibility

A certificate is included only where the programme states it. Learners must complete the required lessons and any published attendance, assignment, project or assessment requirements, and settle the agreed programme fees. Requirements should be disclosed before enrolment. Payment or registration alone does not qualify a learner for a completion certificate.

3. Issuance and timing

For dashboard-tracked courses, certificate records are generated when the required lesson progress reaches completion. If a programme includes additional mentor or project checks, those must also be completed. Where manual issuance or review is required, allow up to seven business days after all stated requirements and learner details are confirmed. If a certificate is missing, contact support with your registered email and programme details.

4. How to request verification

Send the certificate number, course title and a copy or link to the certificate to thecodemunk@gmail.com with the subject “Certificate verification”. You may provide a learner-shared portfolio link as supporting context. TCM will check the issued record and aim to respond within seven business days. Payment receipts and screenshots of a course page are not certificates.

Verification is handled through official support. We do not currently promise an instant public QR-code portal, a blockchain credential or cryptographic signature. Avoid relying on an unofficial verification website.

5. Information disclosed

Verification is limited to the authenticity of the record, programme, issue details and learner name where appropriate. We may request the learner’s authorisation before disclosing personal details to a third party. We do not disclose private contact, payment, assessment or account records merely because someone supplies a certificate number.

6. Corrections, replacement and misuse

Request spelling corrections from your registered email, quoting the certificate number and the correction needed. We may ask for proportionate identity evidence. We will confirm the replacement process and any applicable fee before proceeding; a simple correction does not require buying the course again. Forged, altered or fraudulently obtained credentials can be investigated and withdrawn after review. A corrected or withdrawn record may supersede an earlier version.

7. Support

Keep a copy of your issued certificate. We do not promise indefinite hosting or a particular employer’s acceptance of a credential. Contact us if you need help with an issue, correction or verification request.
