TRUST & TRANSPARENCY

Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your personal information and handling your data with rigorous security, explicit consent, and complete transparency.

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 DPDP Act 2023 Compliant Entity: The Code Munk (TCM One)

1. Introduction

Welcome to The Code Munk ("TCM One", "we", "our", or "us"). Your privacy is paramount to us. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal information when you visit our website (thecodemunk.in), enroll in our programs, join our developer cohorts, or interact with our admissions and learning services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to provide industry-grade learning experiences, mentor guidance, and institutional operations:

  • Personal Identification Data: Full name, email address, phone number, college/institution, and branch when registering or requesting program consultation.
  • Account & Enrollment Data: Enrolled courses, GitHub repository submissions, attendance records, milestone reviews, and verified certification records.
  • Payment Information: Transaction identifiers and payment confirmation receipts processed via encrypted, RBI/PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. We never store full credit card numbers or bank credentials on our servers.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: IP address, device identifiers, browser type, referral source, and learning platform engagement metrics.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for legitimate educational, operational, and certification purposes:

  • Delivering live sessions, hands-on repositories, mentor feedback, and project reviews.
  • Issuing cryptographically verifiable certificates of completion and career credentials.
  • Notifying enrolled learners of batch schedules, live AMA links, and curriculum updates.
  • Connecting students with verified hiring partners for placement drives (solely with explicit learner opt-in).
  • Enhancing course syllabi, curriculum efficacy, and platform usability based on learner feedback.

4. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (SSL/TLS), strict role-based access controls, and regular security vulnerability assessments to protect your data. Your personal details are retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill educational requirements, provide lifetime alumni credential verification, or satisfy mandatory statutory compliance obligations.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We never sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties for commercial advertising. We share limited, relevant data only with:

  • Verified corporate hiring partners when you elect to participate in placement referral drives.
  • Essential operational infrastructure providers (e.g., secure cloud hosting, automated transactional email delivery) bound by strict confidentiality and data protection agreements.
  • Statutory authorities when strictly mandated by applicable laws or valid judicial orders.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We use essential session tokens and cookies to maintain active authentication, remember theme preferences, and analyze aggregate, anonymized traffic. You may disable cookies through your browser settings, though certain authenticated features of the learning portal may not function properly.

7. Your Rights & Choices

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Request a copy or export of your personal data stored on our systems.
  • Request correction or updating of any inaccurate personal records.
  • Withdraw consent from non-transactional communications at any time.
  • Request the complete erasure of your profile and data (subject to mandatory tax and certification retention periods).

For questions, privacy grievances, or data subject requests, please reach out to our dedicated data protection team:
