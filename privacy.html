TCM ONE · LEARNER INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

How The Code Munk handles learner information, payments, analytics, support requests and certificate records.

Effective: 15 October 2026The Code Munk · TCM One
Terms · Privacy · Refunds & cancellation · Course delivery · Internships & placements · Certificates

1. Who we are

This policy describes how The Code Munk, operating the TCM One platform at thecodemunk.in, handles information relating to learners, visitors, applicants and people contacting us. For privacy questions, correction requests or grievances, use the contact details below.

2. Information we handle

Depending on the services you use, we collect your name, email, phone number, account details, institution information, enquiries, course enrolments, lesson progress, assignments and certificate records. Internship applications may include a résumé and information you choose to submit. Payment records may include the amount, transaction reference, status and a receipt or screenshot submitted by you. Avoid including unrelated financial information in screenshots.

Our website and hosting systems may receive IP addresses, browser/device information, logs and usage events. Optional profile, portfolio and community information is visible according to the feature you use; review what you publish before sharing a portfolio link.

3. Why we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, respond to enquiries, verify payments, deliver courses, send learning and payment updates, maintain progress and certificates, investigate misuse and meet applicable recordkeeping duties. Career applications are used to assess the opportunities you request. Promotional messages should be optional; you can ask us to stop them without stopping essential course or payment messages.

4. Payments and service providers

Where an external gateway such as Razorpay is offered, payment information is processed by that provider under its own privacy notice. TCM does not request or store full card numbers, CVVs, banking passwords or payment OTPs. Transaction records and learner-uploaded receipts may be retained for payment reconciliation and disputes.

We use hosting, email, analytics, communication and learning-service providers as needed to operate the platform. WhatsApp and other linked services have their own terms. We may share relevant application information with a prospective employer when you request or authorise a referral, and disclose information when required by law. We do not sell personal information.

5. Cookies, analytics and optional tools

Session cookies support login and security. Browser storage may remember preferences and support platform features. The website uses Google Analytics to measure visits and interactions; this involves browser/device information and analytics identifiers, rather than a guarantee of anonymous browsing. Your browser can block cookies or tracking, although some functions may be affected. Embedded maps and third-party media can receive technical information when loaded.

If you use an optional AI assistant, your messages and relevant learning context may be processed by the configured AI service provider to generate a response. Do not enter passwords, payment credentials or sensitive documents in assistant chats.

6. Retention and protection

We retain information for as long as needed for the service, account administration, certificate verification, payment reconciliation, dispute handling or applicable legal duties. Retention depends on the record and purpose; we do not promise indefinite storage. When no longer needed, records should be deleted or de-identified, subject to required retention and backup cycles. We use access controls and other reasonable safeguards; no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure.

7. Your requests and choices

Contact us from your registered email to request access, correction, account deletion, withdrawal of optional consent or help with a privacy concern. We may verify your identity before disclosing or changing records. We will explain if information must be retained for a legal obligation or unresolved transaction. You can request removal of optional public profile information and stop promotional communications. We handle requests in accordance with applicable Indian data-protection requirements.

8. Children and policy changes

People under 18 should use our services only with parent or guardian authorisation arranged through support. If you believe a child has supplied information without appropriate authorisation, contact us for review. We update this notice when our practices change and show the effective date on this page.
