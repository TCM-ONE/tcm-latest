GOVERNANCE & COMMERCIAL POLICY

Institutional Partnership Policy

Operational standards, attribution tracking, financial settlement milestones, and governance framework governing institutional collaborations with The Code Munk (TCM One).

Version: 2.4 · Last Updated: October 2026 · Jurisdiction: Bilaspur / Raipur, CG, India
Transparency & Legal Notice: This document outlines the operational and commercial principles of the TCM One Institutional Partnership Program. Specific financial shares, cohort dates, infrastructure access, and custom deliverables are formalized via a signed Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) executed between authorized representatives of both institutions.

1. Institutional Eligibility & Accreditation Standards

TCM One partners with registered degree colleges, universities, autonomous institutions, technical training academies, and recognized coaching centers. To participate in our partnership programs:

  • The institution must be a legally recognized educational or vocational entity operating in good standing.
  • An authorized Institutional Coordinator (Director, Principal, HoD, or Placement Officer) must be nominated as the single point of contact.
  • Institutions must demonstrate adequate student interest and the operational commitment required to support cohort learning.
  • Zero Upfront Licensing applies to curriculum and LMS software access; institutions remain responsible for their own internal premise costs, computers, and utility bills.

2. Roles & Operational Governance

Responsibilities are governed strictly by the chosen partnership model:

  • Referral Model: The partner institution facilitates student discovery and directs applicants via institutional referral channels. TCM One assumes full pedagogical delivery, student onboarding, live sessions, AI assistance, assignments, and career support.
  • Academic Partnership Model: A joint co-delivery framework where TCM One provides standardized curriculum, platform access, projects, assessments, and mentor orientation. The partner institution provides physical classroom/lab infrastructure, monitors student attendance, and assigns faculty mentors (where faculty delivery is elected).

3. Referral Tracking & Student Attribution

Transparent attribution is critical to our partner relationships:

  • Upon MoU confirmation, each institutional partner receives a dedicated institutional identifier (Partner Code) and custom landing link.
  • Enrollments originating from the partner portal, institutional registration drive, or verified student list are automatically attributed to the partner's ledger.
  • Lead attribution windows remain valid for 90 days from the initial student registration date.

4. Revenue Sharing & Commercial Calculation

Commercial structures are milestone-based, verified, and settled in Indian National Rupees (INR):

  • Referral Commission: 15% of net course fee collection per confirmed paid enrollment.
  • Academic Partnership Share: 40% Institute / 60% TCM One, calculated on the mutually agreed per-student fee multiplied by the enrolled batch count.
  • All fee collections are net of statutory Goods and Services Tax (GST) and standard payment gateway processing charges (where paid through public online gateways).
  • Any institutional scholarships or concessions agreed upon in writing are deducted from the gross collection prior to share calculation.

5. Payout Settlement Timelines & Tax Deductions

  • Batch Lock Date: Student enrollments for a cohort freeze 14 calendar days after batch commencement.
  • Disbursement Timeline: Approved partner payouts are processed by the 15th of the following month, subject to reconciliation and the approved policy.
  • Settlement Method: Payouts are transferred via direct NEFT/RTGS to the verified institutional bank account accompanied by a detailed batch settlement statement.
  • Tax Deductions: Applicable Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) under Section 194 of the Income Tax Act, 1961 is deducted as mandated by law, and Form 16A certificates are issued quarterly.

6. Student Dropouts, Refunds & Cancellations

  • Students who officially request a refund within the permitted 7-day initial orientation window are refunded according to the TCM One Student Refund Policy.
  • Partner share is not accrued on refunded enrollments.
  • If a student drops out after the 14-day lock date, the institutional share remains credited, as platform licenses and mentor allocations have been committed.

7. Intellectual Property & Curriculum Rights

  • All curriculum syllabi, lesson slide decks, recorded video lectures, project boilerplates, source codes, and AI learning frameworks remain the sole intellectual property of The Code Munk.
  • The partner institution is granted a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the material solely for authorized enrolled batches during the term of the agreement.
  • Reproduction, commercial reselling, or public dissemination of TCM One learning assets outside enrolled cohorts is strictly prohibited.

8. Student Data Privacy (DPDP Act Compliance)

  • Both parties agree to treat student personal information in strict compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.
  • Student contact information and academic records are utilized strictly for platform access, progress tracking, and career placement support.
  • TCM One does not sell student data to third-party commercial advertisers under any circumstances.

9. Agreement Term, Modifications & Termination

  • Institutional partnerships are executed for an initial term of one (1) academic year, renewable by mutual written consent.
  • Either party may terminate the partnership with sixty (60) days prior written notice, subject to the condition that all active enrolled student batches must be completed without disruption.
  • Disputes arising out of the partnership shall be resolved through friendly executive consultation, and if unresolved, subject to the jurisdiction of courts in Bilaspur / Raipur, Chhattisgarh.
