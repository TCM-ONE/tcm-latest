GOVERNANCE & COMMERCIAL POLICY

Institutional Partnership Policy

Operational standards, attribution tracking, financial settlement milestones, and governance framework governing institutional collaborations with TCM One.

Version: 2.4 (Updated 2026) The Code Munk (TCM One) India Jurisdiction
Transparency & Legal Notice: This document outlines the operational and commercial principles of the TCM One Institutional Partnership Program. Specific financial shares, cohort dates, infrastructure access, and custom deliverables are formalized via a signed Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) executed between authorized representatives of both institutions.

1. Institutional Eligibility & Standards

TCM One partners with registered degree colleges, universities, autonomous institutes, technical training academies, and recognized coaching centers. To participate:

  • The institution must be a legally recognized educational or vocational entity operating in good standing.
  • An authorized Institutional Coordinator (Director, Principal, HoD, or Placement Officer) must be nominated as the single point of contact.
  • Institutions must demonstrate adequate student interest and the operational commitment required to support cohort learning.
  • Zero Upfront Licensing applies to curriculum and LMS software access; institutions remain responsible for their own internal premise costs, computers, and utility bills.

2. Roles & Operational Governance

Responsibilities are governed strictly by the chosen partnership model:

  • Referral Model: The partner institution facilitates student discovery and directs applicants via institutional referral channels. TCM One assumes full pedagogical delivery, student onboarding, live sessions, AI assistance, assignments, and career support.
  • Academic Partnership Model: A joint co-delivery framework where TCM One provides standardized curriculum, platform access, projects, assessments, and mentor orientation. The partner institution provides physical classroom/lab infrastructure, monitors student attendance, and assigns faculty mentors.

3. Referral Tracking & Student Attribution

Transparent attribution is critical to our partner relationships:

  • Upon MoU confirmation, each institutional partner receives a dedicated institutional identifier (Partner Code) and custom landing link.
  • Enrollments originating from the partner portal, institutional registration drive, or verified student list are automatically attributed to the partner's ledger.
  • Lead attribution windows remain valid for 90 days from the initial student registration date.

4. Revenue Sharing & Commercial Calculation

Commercial structures are milestone-based, verified, and settled in Indian National Rupees (INR):

  • Referral Commission: 15% of net course fee collection per confirmed paid enrollment.
  • Academic Partnership Share: 40% Institute / 60% TCM One, calculated on the mutually agreed per-student fee multiplied by the enrolled batch count.
  • All fee collections are net of statutory Goods and Services Tax (GST) and standard payment gateway processing charges.

5. Payout Settlement Timelines

  • Batch Lock Date: Student enrollments for a cohort freeze 14 calendar days after batch commencement.
  • Disbursement Timeline: Approved partner payouts are processed by the 15th of the following month, subject to reconciliation.
  • Settlement Method: Payouts are transferred via direct NEFT/RTGS accompanied by a detailed batch settlement statement.
  • TDS Compliance: Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) is deducted as mandated by law, and Form 16A certificates are issued quarterly.

6. Intellectual Property & Curriculum Rights

  • All curriculum syllabi, slide decks, recorded lectures, project repositories, and AI frameworks remain the sole intellectual property of The Code Munk.
  • The partner institution is granted a non-exclusive license solely for authorized enrolled batches during the agreement term.
