THE INDUSTRY GAP

What Problem Are We Solving?

Many educational institutions face similar systemic challenges when updating technical training:

Students complete courses but lack practical hands-on exposure.

Mentors have subject knowledge but need structured curriculum and teaching resources.

Developing new curriculum internally takes significant time and heavy financial resources.

Students lack access to 24×7 learning support outside classroom hours.

Career guidance is often disconnected from day-to-day academic learning.

Institutes lack the technology infrastructure required for modern digital learning.

Employer Expectations Shift: Employers increasingly demand verified skills, practical projects, and real capability rather than traditional certificates alone.

PARTNERSHIP OPTIONS

Our Partnership Models

Choose the operational model that best fits your institution's infrastructure, faculty bandwidth, and strategic goals:

MODEL 1

Referral Model

Refer Students. Zero Faculty Overhead. Transparent Monthly Payouts.

Built for colleges, training centers, and academic organizations that want to offer high-demand career tracks without taking operational responsibility for daily classroom delivery.

Who is this model suitable for?
Colleges, degree academies, polytechnics, and skill communities wanting to test student demand with zero operational burden.
  • Institute Role: Promote courses, share verified institutional link, counsel interested students.
  • TCM One Role: Complete curriculum, live sessions, 24×7 Ask AI doubt clearing, assignments & certificates.
  • Referral Tracking: Dedicated Institutional Partner Code and portal attribution ledger.
  • Commercial Commission: 15% – 25% commission per verified, paid student enrollment.
  • Payout Eligibility: Monthly automated bank transfer within 30 days of cohort lock date.
MODEL 2 · MOST POPULAR

Academic Partnership Model

Co-Deliver Accredited Programs on Campus · 35%–50% Revenue Share

A structured institutional collaboration where the institute and TCM One co-deliver full-stack, AI, or data programs to dedicated student cohorts with co-branded recognition.

Cohort Eligibility & Lab Standards:
Minimum batch size: 20 students. Campus lab requires 1 PC per student, projector, and 20Mbps+ stable broadband.
  • Flexible Mentorship: Nominated faculty trained by TCM One or dedicated TCM industry mentors.
  • Institute Role: Lab scheduling, internal attendance monitoring, and coordinator supervision.
  • TCM One Role: Full curriculum, project repositories, LMS access, AI engine, and career placement support.
  • Revenue-Sharing: 35% – 50% institutional revenue share based on delivery allocation.
  • Settlement Process: Milestone-based ledger with transparent batch enrollment audits and quarterly TDS certificates.
