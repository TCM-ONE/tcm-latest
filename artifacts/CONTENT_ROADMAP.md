## 3. On-Page Article Blueprint ("blogs bhi seo hone chahiye")

Each published article must adhere to this structural formula:

1. **Title Tag**: `[Article Title] — TCM One Tech Updates` (under 60 characters).
2. **Meta Description**: Direct answer or compelling hook containing primary keyword (140–155 characters).
3. **Featured Image**: 1200x630 WebP/PNG thumbnail with descriptive `alt` text.
4. **Author Byline**: "TCM One Editorial" with link to `/about`.
5. **Table of Contents / Overview**: Bulleted summary of the 4–6 key sections for quick skim reading.
6. **Code Blocks & Architectural Snippets**: Clean, syntax-highlighted code with real-world context (no pseudo-code placeholders).
7. **Interactive Elements**: Like button and verified student comment threads to generate authentic E-E-A-T engagement signals.
8. **Conversion Action Box**: Distinct editorial callout card:
   > **Want Mentor Guidance on This Topic?**  
   > Master these practical concepts in our live cohort with direct code reviews.  
   > [Explore the [Course Title] &rarr;](/courses/[slug])
