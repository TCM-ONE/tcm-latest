CLARITY

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about learning modes, batch schedules, fees, and enrollment.

Are classes live or pre-recorded?
All TCM One courses and workshops are conducted 100% live online. Sessions include interactive coding, real-time question answering, and screen shares. Recordings are also archived for later review.
What prerequisites do I need to get started?
Our beginner programs (such as Python Foundations and Frontend Web Development) require no prior coding experience. All you need is a laptop, stable internet connection, and curiosity.
How does course enrollment and payment work?
You can select any course or workshop, click Reserve Seat or Enroll, and complete registration securely. For courses above ₹5,000, flexible installment arrangements are available upon request.
Will I receive a verified certificate upon completion?
Yes. Learners who complete the required project deliverables and attend the scheduled cohort receive an official, shareable certificate of completion from TCM One.
