FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our courses, live learning, mentors, and placement support.

How are TCM One courses structured?
All programs feature 100% live instructor-led masterclasses, industry-grade hands-on capstone projects, weekly assessments, and continuous doubt clearing with dedicated tech mentors.
Do I need prior coding knowledge before joining?
Not at all. We teach from absolute ground zero. Concepts are built step-by-step from foundational logic to real-world deployment, making it suitable for beginners and college students.
What if I miss a live class?
Every live session is recorded and uploaded to your student portal within 2 to 4 hours. You receive lifetime access to class recordings, source code, and project repositories.
What is the Phlappy Course Bucket free benefit?
When you enroll in any flagship track, you receive complimentary full access to Phlappy's complete masterclass library, curated project roadmaps, and community perks at zero additional cost.
Will I receive certificates and placement support?
Yes, upon project completion you receive verified certificates, plus tailored resume reviews, LinkedIn/GitHub portfolio optimization, and referral opportunities across hiring partner companies.
How can I clear doubts during and after class?
You can ask live during sessions, join daily mentor breakout rooms, or post in our private 24/7 Discord & Slack developer community where instructors respond actively.
